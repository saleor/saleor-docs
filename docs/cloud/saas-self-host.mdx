Saleor’s commerce core is open source. You can run it yourself or use Saleor Cloud to have Saleor operate the core and its managed services. Choose based on the system you want to operate, not only where the API runs.

## Responsibilities

| Area                              | Saleor Cloud                                                                                 | Self-hosting                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Core, database and infrastructure | Saleor operates and maintains the hosted environment.                                        | Your team provisions, operates, monitors and backs up the deployment.                                       |
| Updates and scaling               | Saleor manages Cloud updates and infrastructure scaling.                                     | Your team plans and runs upgrades and capacity changes.                                                     |
| Storefront                        | Use Paper or another frontend; host on Engine or externally.                                 | Connect Paper or another frontend and arrange hosting.                                                      |
| Custom apps and integrations      | Your team owns its code; host on Engine or externally.                                       | Your team builds, deploys and operates custom code.                                                         |
| Managed apps and services         | Cloud includes Engine, Pulse, Essential Apps and support.                                    | Cloud-only services do not transfer with the core. Choose open-source alternatives or provide replacements. |
| Implementation help               | Optional Accelerator and Made-to-Measure with monthly payment; included with annual payment. | Your team or implementation partner delivers the system.                                                    |

## Code and Data Ownership

You own your storefront, apps, integrations and commerce data. Saleor retains ownership of the underlying platform and Cloud infrastructure. The open-source core remains available under its license.

Moving between Cloud and self-hosting is supported, but requires planning. Check version compatibility, data migration and replacements for Cloud-only services. A Cloud-to-self-hosted move includes work your team must operate after migration; it is not a transfer of Saleor’s managed infrastructure.
